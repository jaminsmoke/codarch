**What it is**: **declarative knowledge** —profiles, rules and saved views— that is installed by
copying a folder and travels with the repo.

**Why**: a team working with a framework does not want a new view: it wants its layers, its rules
and a reasonable starting view from day one, and for its teammates and its CI to have them too.
Today that is a README with a block to copy.

**Decision**: [ADR 0029](../../adr/0021-0030/0029-los-addons.md). An addon adds **no** code (that is
a plugin, ADR 0028) and no lenses (that would change the checksum). It lives in
`addons/<name>/` and the repo declares it in `.codarch.yml`.

**The boundary, in one line**: a **plugin** brings **code**; an **addon** brings **knowledge**; a
**lens** brings **measurements** and it is the only one that touches the checksum.

**Verdict**: **phase M16**, in the roadmap of
[ADR 0029](../../adr/0021-0030/0029-los-addons.md). It started here and it is already a phase: the
trace of why stays.
