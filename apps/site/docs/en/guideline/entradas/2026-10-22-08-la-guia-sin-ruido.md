---
title: The guide, without noise
date: "2026-10-22"
category: fix
verdict: arreglo
---

**What it was**: the guide had accumulated a "done" symbol behind almost every word. An icon that
repeats in every sentence stops meaning anything: it covers the text, dirties the reading and makes
the page look like a draft.

**What was done**: only the legend icons remain, where they explain something. The body goes
without marks and states are said in words: "done", "pending", "fixed". The page separates days by
category —features, fixes, docs, maintenance— and each group has its color and its count; inside,
each entry has its verdict label, its typography and its breathing room.

**Why**: a guide is read. The record is already in the markdown; the site only has to make it
readable.

**Verdict**: **fix**, no phase. It changes neither the model nor the checksum.
