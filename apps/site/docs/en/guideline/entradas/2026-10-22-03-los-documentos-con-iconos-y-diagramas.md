**What it is**: documents that are not a wall of plain text. A markdown without signals reads like a
wall: decisions get buried.

**Decision**: [ADR 0031](../../adr/0031-0040/0031-los-documentos.md) (its §6). A **small** set of
icons with their legend —category and state— and **mermaid** where a diagram saves a paragraph.

**Verdict**: **phase M19**, in the roadmap of
[ADR 0031](../../adr/0031-0040/0031-los-documentos.md). Done.
