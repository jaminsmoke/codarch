---
title: The documents, as an index
date: "2026-10-23"
category: docs
verdict: arreglo
---

**What it was**: the documents page opened with two grids of cards —product and decisions—.
Everything weighed the same and the reader had to enter every card to know what was there.

**What was done**: it is an editorial index now. The product reads as numbered chapters, in rows
with their description; the decisions are a timeline in blocks of ten, with the number, the title
and its changelog. Cards are gone: typography and rules lead.

**Why**: a documents page is browsed. The structure has to let you see what is inside before going
in.

**Decision**: [ADR 0034](../../adr/0031-0040/0034-la-web-del-proyecto.md). It changes neither the
model nor the checksum: it changes how it reads.

**Verdict**: **fix**, inside M23.
