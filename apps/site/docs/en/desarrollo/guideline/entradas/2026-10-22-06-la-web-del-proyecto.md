**What it is**: the repo having a **front door**: what CodArch is, how it looks, how it is installed
and the documents **browsable**.

**Decision**: [ADR 0034](../../adr/0031-0040/0034-la-web-del-proyecto.md). A site in `apps/site`
(React + Vite + router) that **is generated from the markdown** —a single truth— and is published on
GitHub Pages.

**Verdict**: **phase M23**, in the roadmap of
[ADR 0034](../../adr/0031-0040/0034-la-web-del-proyecto.md#hoja-de-ruta). The site is published at
`jaminsmoke.github.io/codarch`.
