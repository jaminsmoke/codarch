CodArch is explained in two blocks (ADR 0080):

- **Using CodArch**: what someone installing and using it needs. The [installation](usar/instalacion.md),
  [getting started](usar/primeros-pasos.md), the CLI with one page per command (start with
  [`codarch analyze`](usar/cli/analyze.md)), the [web app](usar/webapp.md), the [desktop app](usar/escritorio.md), the
  [`.codarch.yml` rules](rules.md), the [MCP](mcp.md), the [GitHub Action](usar/github-action.md),
  [updates](usar/actualizaciones.md) and the [FAQ](usar/preguntas-frecuentes.md).
- **How it is built**: what explains the product from the inside. The [roadmap](adr/roadmap.md), the
  [data model](model.md), the [map design](design/map.md), the [migration to Rust](migration.md),
  the decisions (each ADR in its folder of ten, with its roadmap inside; start with the
  [decision index](adr/index.md)) and the [guide](/guia/), which keeps what is not a decision yet:
  ideas, fixes and what is waiting.
